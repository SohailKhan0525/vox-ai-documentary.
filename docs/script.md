# AI can draft. Who checks?

AI can draft an answer in seconds. But who checks it?

A job isn't one task. It's a bundle.

Reading. Writing. Deciding. AI can help with parts of that bundle.

But a fluent answer can still be wrong.

And when mistakes move faster, checking matters more.

So the question isn't just what we can automate.

It's what we choose to keep human.
