# Creative direction: The work after the answer

**Central question:** If AI can produce an answer, what work remains for us?

**Editorial thesis:** Generating and verifying are different tasks. The future of useful AI depends on how people combine them. This is a focused editorial proposition, not a prediction of a specific employment outcome.

**Original identity:** An intelligent printed field guide, warm cream and deep ink, with teal for generation/process and coral for uncertainty/review. Strong grotesk headlines, editorial serif accents, monospaced labels. No Vox marks, footage, copied layouts or music.

**Hero motif:** One answer card. It begins as a confident-looking output, unfolds into a task diagram, acquires a visible question mark, passes through verification and resolves into a human decision. Reuse this object across cuts so the story has visual continuity.

**Composition:** 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, exactly 1,050 frames. Important material should generally remain within x=90–930 and y=220–1560; captions and source labels must avoid the far-right social controls and bottom platform overlays. One dominant focal point per scene. Keep supporting type visibly subordinate without reducing it to unreadable tiny print.

**Motion:** Smooth camera and line reveals; purposeful paper-layer offsets. Introduce concepts in sequence, then connect them. Restrained spring settles and 3–5-frame stagger. A transition must move the explanatory object or change the viewer's understanding. Slow down for the key distinction: fluent ≠ verified.

**Sound:** Documentary narration leads. Original restrained electronic pulse/harmonic bed. Soft impact at the opening, paper/marker accents on motivated changes, subtle rising energy into the final insight, a clean resolved ending. No borrowed commercial soundtrack. Measure voice duration before final timing; document local TTS quality honestly.

**Evidence:** Use no unsupported percentages or speculative employment charts. Label schematic answer/process examples as illustration. Explain factual limits in supporting documentation; keep the phone-screen story concise. Source the capability/falsehood distinction to the accessed primary documents in VOX_STYLE_RESEARCH.md.

**Prototype gate:** First five seconds must include a compelling answer-object composition, at least three independent moving layers, a real attention-carrying transition, narration, an original music bed and a timed accent. Inspect representative frames before committing to the entire system. Direct frame-by-frame comparison with Vox remains unavailable; compare against the documented principles and inspected secondary examples with that limitation explicit.

**Final narration:** “AI can draft an answer in seconds. But who checks it? A job isn't one task. It's a bundle. Reading. Writing. Deciding. AI can help with parts of that bundle. But a fluent answer can still be wrong. And when mistakes move faster, checking matters more. So the question isn't just what we can automate. It's what we choose to keep human.” The job-task diagram is schematic; the conclusion is an editorial question, not a forecast.

## Implemented system

Final palette: warm cream `#eeece2`, ink `#142e35`, lime `#d9f56a`, coral `#e5674e`. DejaVu Sans bold/regular and DejaVu Sans Mono are locally bundled. No teal process fill or external imagery is required. Final scene transitions use an incoming illustrated paper page rather than a full-frame solid-color flash. The delivered final is seven scenes, not every possible treatment described above.
